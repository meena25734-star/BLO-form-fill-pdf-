1. Zero Data Collection, No Login & Zero Server Uploads

We do not require user login and do not collect, transmit, store on any cloud server, sell, or share any personal information entered into this app. All form entries (applicant names, relative names, addresses, Aadhaar/EPIC numbers, dates of birth, photographs, signatures, and BLO seal images) stay strictly inside your device to compile your PDF locally.

2. Camera Permission Usage

The app requests access to your device camera solely when you tap Capture Photo or Capture Signature to photograph a passport-size portrait or an applicant's signature/thumb impression on white paper. Captured frames are processed locally on your device and embedded directly into your generated PDF file. No camera feed or image is ever uploaded to any external server.

3. Offline Local Storage (BLO Profile, Drafts & Saved Forms List)

To work 100% offline without login and prevent data loss:

  • BLO Default Profile & Active Draft are stored locally in your device's browser storage (localStorage) so constituency details and BLO seal auto-fill without retyping.
  • Offline Saved Forms List is stored exclusively in your device's local IndexedDB database so you can review, edit, download PDFs, or export a local CSV register without internet access.
  • Blank PDF Templates are cached locally via Service Worker / CacheStorage so PDF compilation and preview work offline.

You remain in complete control of all local records and can permanently delete your BLO Default Profile, saved drafts, or any saved form entry at any time inside the app.
